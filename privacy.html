Summary

The short versionWe collect what you put into Ocufuse so the app can work for you. Ocufuse is for adults aged 18 or older. We ask for your explicit consent before you log health information, and eye photos have their own consent that you can withdraw. We store your data in the EU (Ireland). We don't sell it, advertise with it, or use it to train AI. If you subscribe to Ocufuse Plus, Apple (iPhone) or Stripe (web) takes the payment, and we never see your card number. You can export or delete your data at any time.

This policy explains what personal data Ocufuse collects, why, where it is kept, who else handles it, and the rights you have. It covers the Ocufuse apps (iPhone and web) and this website.

The games list

To unlock Shade Hunt, Dot Chase and Edge Spot on ocufuse.com, you give us your email address and agree to game emails. We use it only to send news about Ocufuse games and updates, at most twice a month, and never sell or share it for advertising. It is sent through our form relay, FormSubmit, and kept in our email inbox. Every email has a way to unsubscribe, or write to hello@ocufuse.com and we remove you. We keep it until you unsubscribe. Whether the games are unlocked is remembered only in your browser.

What we don't collect

  • We don't track your location. An appointment's place is only the place you pick.
  • We don't read or upload your address book, and we don't read data from Apple's Health app.
  • We don't collect advertising identifiers or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The apps have no advertising tools and no third-party analytics. The web app keeps anonymous daily totals of how often a few features are used, such as a game finished or a photo saved. Nothing that identifies you, your account, your device or your IP address is stored with these totals.
  • We don't run facial recognition and don't create face templates or biometric identifiers from your photos (see Eye photos and on-device analysis).
  • The website uses Google Analytics only if you accept it in the cookie banner. Without your consent it sets no cookies and loads no tracking.

Health information and your consent

Your eye photos, journal entries, care records and prescriptions are information about your health. Data protection law treats this as a special category of personal data, and some US state laws treat it as consumer health data or sensitive personal information. We only process it with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)).

When you set up Ocufuse, the consent screen asks you to:

  • confirm that you are 18 or older;
  • accept the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy;
  • give your explicit consent for us to store and use your health information to run Ocufuse for you. This is required, because keeping that record is what Ocufuse does;
  • decide separately whether to allow eye photos. You can withdraw this consent at any time in the app. Withdrawing it deletes your eye photos.

We record your choices on your profile, with the date and the version of the wording you agreed to.

To withdraw your consent for health information, delete the entries you no longer want us to keep, delete your account (Profile & settings > Data & privacy > Delete account), or email us and we will delete it for you. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before.

We do not use your health information for advertising, sell it, or share it with anyone except the providers who host and run Ocufuse for us (see Who handles your data). We do not use it to train AI models.

Eye photos and on-device analysis

  • Your photos. Before upload, each photo is resized and saved again as a new image, which removes hidden camera data such as the location where it was taken. Photos are kept in private storage, in a folder that belongs to your account. Only you can see them through the app.
  • Finding your eyes. In the iPhone app, the camera guide and the eye markers use Apple's face landmark detection, which runs on your iPhone, to find where your eyes are. It does not create or store a face template or anything used to recognise who you are, and it is never used to identify you.
  • Marker points. We save only a few points on each photo, such as the centre of each pupil, the light reflection, the eyelid edges and the eye corners, as positions on the image, so you can see and adjust them later. They are deleted with the photo or your account. To show your photos quickly, your iPhone may also keep eye positions on the phone only; they are removed when you log out.
  • No diagnosis. Ocufuse does not diagnose or measure your eyes. Photo-derived estimates are never shown in the App Store version.

Eye prescriptions

You can add an eye prescription by typing it in, scanning a paper copy with your camera, or importing a photo or PDF. When you scan or import one, the text is read on your iPhone to fill in the form; it is not sent anywhere to be read. You check the values before you save them.

We store the values you save. We keep the original photo or PDF only if you choose to, in your account's private storage. Deleting a prescription deletes its file too.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store your photos and entries and show them back to you: your timeline, comparisons, checklist, practice log, level and points.
  • To build a Doctor Brief or a records export (PDF, CSV or text) when you ask for one. On iPhone, these files are made on your phone and only leave it if you share them.
  • To provide Ocufuse Plus: check whether your account has an active subscription, trial or lifetime purchase, unlock Plus on both iPhone and web, and answer billing and refund questions.
  • To keep the service working and secure, fix errors, and prevent abuse.
  • To answer your messages and send essential service emails, such as sign-in links and account deletion confirmations.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use your photos or entries to train machine learning models. We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Legal bases

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws, we rely on:

  • Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): to provide your account, the features you use and, if you subscribe, Ocufuse Plus.
  • Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)): to store and process your health information, including eye photos, journal entries, care records and prescriptions. See Health information and your consent.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, answer messages you send us, and keep the care contacts you choose to save.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must keep or disclose information by law, such as tax and accounting records for subscriptions.

You can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing your eye-photo consent deletes your photos. Because Ocufuse cannot work without the health information you add, withdrawing that consent means we delete it, or your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before.

Payments

On iPhone, Ocufuse Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds under its own terms and privacy policy. We use RevenueCat to check purchases: the app gives RevenueCat your Ocufuse account ID (a random code, not your name or email) together with your purchase history from Apple and technical data such as app version, device type and IP address. RevenueCat then tells our server your subscription status.

On the web, Stripe takes the payment for Ocufuse Plus and for one-off tips. Stripe receives your card details, billing country and payment history; we receive your subscription status and a customer reference.

We never see your card or bank details.

International transfers

Your account, photos, journal entries and other records are stored in the EU (Ireland). Ocufuse is run from Kosovo, and some providers above are based in the United States or work worldwide, so data may be accessed from outside your country. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU or European Economic Area, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum for UK data).

How long we keep it

  • Account, photos and entries: until you delete them or your account. When you delete an entry or photo, it is removed from the live database and storage straight away.
  • Eye photos after you withdraw photo consent: deleted straight away.
  • After you delete your account: your login, profile, every entry and every file in your account's folders (photos, prescriptions and appointment images) are removed from live systems straight away.
  • On your iPhone: cached photos, settings and export files are removed when you log out or delete your account, and when you delete the app.
  • Security logs: up to 90 days.
  • Subscription and payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (often up to 10 years), even after you delete your account. These records hold billing details only, never your photos or entries. Apple, RevenueCat and Stripe keep their own purchase records as their terms and the law require.
  • Messages to us: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.
  • Inactive accounts: if you don't sign in for 24 months, we email you, and delete the account 30 days later if you don't respond.

When you delete your account, we erase your personal data within 30 days, including from backups. The exceptions are: security logs, kept up to 90 days to protect the service; messages you send us, kept up to 24 months so we can answer follow-ups; and anything the law requires us to keep (for example records of purchases for tax and accounting), kept only as long as required. We use these for nothing else.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it first in your iPhone's Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access rules in the database (row level security) mean each account can only read and change its own records, and files are kept in private storage, with one folder per account, that requires your signed-in session. The keys that can get around these rules exist only on our server, never in the app. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.

To prevent abuse, we limit how often sign-in can be tried, how many emails can be sent and how many entries can be added, and we limit the size of uploads. On iPhone, cached photos and export files use iOS's strongest file protection, so they can't be read while your phone is locked. Access to production systems is limited to keeping the service running or helping you when you ask.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the supervisory authority as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Delete your data, which you can do yourself from the app (see how). Cancel any Ocufuse Plus subscription first: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store.
  • Receive your data in a portable format. The app's export gives you your entries in a machine-readable file. For a complete copy, including photo files, email us.
  • Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including your separate consent for eye photos (see Health information and your consent).

Email hello@ocufuse.com or use the contact form to make a request. We answer within one month (for complex requests we may need up to two more months, and will tell you why). We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address on your account. Using your rights is free.

Please tell us first if you are unhappy, so we can try to fix it. You can also complain to a data protection authority:

  • Kosovo: Information and Privacy Agency (Agjencia për Informim dhe Privatësi), aip.rks-gov.net
  • EU and EEA: the authority in the country where you live or work (list of authorities)
  • United Kingdom: Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk
  • Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, edoeb.admin.ch

Notices for US residents

We do not sell your personal information, including health information, or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the service you ask for.

California

Under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA: in the past 12 months we collected identifiers (email address, account ID, IP address and, on this website with your consent, cookie IDs); personal records (name); characteristics of protected classifications (age or date of birth and sex, if you add them); commercial information (subscription status and purchase history); internet or network activity (server logs and, with your consent, website analytics); and sensitive personal information (account login and health information). Sources, purposes and retention are described above. We disclose these categories only to the providers in Who handles your data for business purposes. You have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. You can use an authorised agent; we will verify requests through the email address on your account.

Washington, Nevada and other consumer health data laws

Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains how we handle consumer health data under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law and similar laws. We do not sell covered information as defined by Nevada law.

Other US states

If you live in a state such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas or Oregon, you may have the right to access, correct, delete and get a copy of your data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling (we do none of these). You can appeal our decision on your request by replying to it. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General.

Biometric privacy laws

We do not collect biometric identifiers or biometric information to identify you, as covered by laws in states such as Illinois, Texas and Washington. The on-device face landmark detection and the marker points described in Eye photos and on-device analysis are used only to show your eyes on your own photos. They are never used to recognise anyone, never sold, never disclosed except to our hosting provider, and deleted with the photo or your account.

Children

Ocufuse is for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

ocufuse.com is a static website. Our hosting provider, GitHub, records the IP address of each visit in server logs for security. Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads; Google describes how it handles this in its Fonts privacy FAQ. The status line in the footer checks that our services are running by contacting Supabase, which receives your IP address. If you use the contact form, your message is sent through FormSubmit to our inbox.

The free browser games keep your best score in your browser's local storage, together with your light or dark mode, eye comfort mode and cookie choice. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Analytics cookies

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, the website loads Google Analytics 4 to measure which pages and links are used, how far pages are scrolled, which FAQ questions are opened, which free games are started and finished (with the score), and which Plus plan buttons are clicked. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) that last up to two years. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or anything from your Ocufuse account, and the web app at app.ocufuse.com never uses Google Analytics; it only keeps the anonymous daily totals described above. If you click Decline, nothing is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app or by email before the changes take effect, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it. Earlier versions are available on request.
