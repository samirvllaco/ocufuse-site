Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what Ocufuse collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. Ocufuse is operated by Samir Vllaco ("we").

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the sign-in method you use (Apple, Google, email and password, or email link).
  • Eye photos you take in the app.
  • Log entries you add, such as symptoms, eye drops, caffeine, sleep, screen time and notes, with the time you set for each.
  • Settings such as language, theme and reminders.

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Why we use it

Only to run the features you use: showing your timeline, comparing photos, and building a Doctor Brief when you ask for one. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Where it is stored

Your account, log entries and photos are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes the data on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your account unless you export or send it yourself, for example a Doctor Brief you give to your clinician.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app's settings. This removes your account, photos and log entries from our systems. Backups are cleared on their normal cycle, within 30 days.

Children

Ocufuse is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data at any time by writing to us.

Not medical advice

Ocufuse is a journaling tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.
