Summary

The short versionWe collect what you put into Ocufuse so the app can work for you. We store it in the EU. We don't sell it, advertise with it, or use it to train AI. If you subscribe to Ocufuse Plus, Stripe (web) or Apple (iPhone) takes the payment, and we never see your card number. You can export or delete your data at any time.

This policy explains what personal data Ocufuse collects, why, where it is kept, who else handles it, and the rights you have. It covers the Ocufuse apps (iPhone and web) and this website.

Health data

Your eye photos and journal entries can reveal information about your health. Data protection law treats this as a special category of personal data, and we only process it with your explicit consent (see Legal bases).

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't run facial recognition and don't create biometric templates from your photos.
  • The website uses Google Analytics only if you accept it in the cookie banner. Without your consent it sets no cookies and loads no tracking.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store your photos and entries and show them back to you: your timeline, comparisons, checklist and practice log.
  • To build a Doctor Brief or export when you ask for one.
  • To provide Ocufuse Plus: check whether your account has an active subscription or trial, unlock Plus on both iPhone and web, and answer billing and refund questions.
  • To keep the service working and secure, fix errors, and prevent abuse.
  • To answer your messages and send essential service emails, such as sign-in links and account deletion confirmations.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use your photos or entries to train machine learning models. We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Legal bases

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws, we rely on:

  • Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): to provide your account, the features you use and, if you subscribe, Ocufuse Plus.
  • Explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)): to store and process your eye photos and health-related journal entries. We ask for this consent separately when you set up the app, and keep a record of the wording you agreed to and when.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and answer messages you send us.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must keep or disclose information by law, such as tax and accounting records for subscriptions.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account. Because Ocufuse cannot work without the photos and entries you add, withdrawing consent means we delete them. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before.

International transfers

Your account, photos and journal entries are stored in the EU. Some providers above are based in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the EU or European Economic Area, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Account, photos and entries: until you delete them or your account.
  • After you delete your account: removed from live systems straight away and from backups within 30 days.
  • Security logs: up to 90 days.
  • Subscription and payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (often up to 10 years), even after you delete your account. These records hold billing details only, never your photos or entries.
  • Messages to us: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted.
  • Inactive accounts: if you don't sign in for 24 months, we email you, and delete the account 30 days later if you don't respond.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access rules in the database mean each account can only read its own records, and photos are kept in private storage that requires your signed-in session. Access to production systems is limited to keeping the service running or helping you when you ask.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the supervisory authority as the law requires.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong.
  • Delete your data, which you can do yourself from the app (see how). Cancel any Ocufuse Plus subscription first: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store.
  • Receive your data in a portable format. The app's CSV export covers your entries.
  • Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

Email hello@ocufuse.com or use the contact form to make a request. We answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email address on your account.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live or work, or the data protection authority of the country where the operator is established.

Children

Ocufuse is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

ocufuse.com is a static website. Our hosting provider, GitHub, records the IP address of each visit in server logs for security. Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads; Google describes how it handles this in its Fonts privacy FAQ. The status line in the footer checks that our services are running by contacting Supabase, which receives your IP address. If you use the contact form, your message is sent through FormSubmit to our inbox.

The free browser games keep your best score in your browser's local storage, together with your light or dark mode, eye comfort mode and cookie choice. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Analytics cookies

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, the website loads Google Analytics 4 to measure which pages and links are used, how far pages are scrolled, which FAQ questions are opened, which free games are started and finished (with the score), and which Plus plan buttons are clicked. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) that last up to two years. We do not send your name, email address, phone number or anything from your Ocufuse account, and the web app at app.ocufuse.com never uses analytics. If you click Decline, nothing is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app or by email before the changes take effect. Earlier versions are available on request.
