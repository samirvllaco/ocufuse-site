Two minutes a day, one clear record

  1. Take guided photos

    Ocufuse walks you through four views of your eyes, the same way each day, so your photos line up when you compare them later.

  2. Note what happened

    Log caffeine, sleep, screen time, eye drops and how your eyes felt in a tap. Set when it happened: now, 15 minutes ago, an hour ago, or a time you pick.

    Flip through days side by side, then export a Doctor Brief as print, text or CSV for your appointment.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Ocufuse, your photos and your data.

What is Ocufuse?

Ocufuse is a daily eye photo journal for web and iPhone. The app guides you through four consistent eye photos, lets you log timed context such as sleep, caffeine, screen time and eye drops, and builds a Doctor Brief you can print or send to your eye doctor before an appointment.

Is Ocufuse a medical device?

Ocufuse is not a medical device. The app does not diagnose, monitor, treat or prevent any eye condition, and it does not measure eye alignment or label photos as normal or abnormal. It is a personal journal that helps you keep a consistent record to share with a qualified eye care professional.

How much does Ocufuse cost?

Ocufuse is free during early access. The web app is open now at app.ocufuse.com, and the iPhone app follows on the App Store. If paid features are added later, Ocufuse will show the price before any charge, and App Store purchases will be handled by Apple under its standard terms.

Is Ocufuse available on iPhone?

The Ocufuse iPhone app is in development and will be released on the App Store. The web app is open now at app.ocufuse.com and works in any modern browser on phone or desktop. When the iPhone app arrives, you sign in with the same account and your photos and entries carry over.

How do I take a photo of my eye for my doctor?

Take eye photos in soft, even light with the flash off, at a steady distance of about 30 to 40 cm, with your head level. Use the same views each time, such as straight ahead, left, right and up. Ask your clinician whether they want extra views, such as looking down.

Does Ocufuse use my photos to train AI?

Ocufuse does not use your photos or journal entries to train AI or machine learning models. Your data is used only to run the features you use, such as your timeline, comparisons and Doctor Brief. Ocufuse also does not sell data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising.

Where does Ocufuse store my data?

Ocufuse stores account data, eye photos and journal entries in the EU, in Ireland, through its hosting provider Supabase. Photos sit in private storage that only your signed-in account can read. Ocufuse does not sell data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on your photos.

Can I delete my Ocufuse data?

Ocufuse lets you delete your account and all its data from Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Photos, entries and settings are removed from live systems immediately and from encrypted backups within 30 days. Export a Doctor Brief or CSV first if you want to keep a copy.
