Questions people ask.

Is it for me?6 questions
My eyes are just tired from screens. Is this for me?

Yes, that's where most people start. Take a two-minute game or a 20-20-20 break between work sessions and note how your eyes felt. If something changes later, you already have a record to show.

Will Ocufuse fix my eyes?

No, and we won't pretend it will. Ocufuse helps you take screen breaks, follow the exercises your eye doctor gave you, and keep a clear record of how your eyes feel. That record makes appointments more useful. Treatment decisions stay with your eye care professional.

I already see an eye doctor. Why would I need this?

Visits are short and symptoms come and go. Ocufuse keeps a dated log, photos and practice history between visits, then turns it into one page you can show. Your doctor sees the days they couldn't see in the room.

I've tried apps like this and stopped. Why is this different?

There's nothing to set up and nothing to keep up with. A check-in takes about 20 seconds, the games are short enough for a coffee break, and a reminder only nudges you if you haven't opened it that day.

I'm busy. How much time does it really take?

About 20 seconds to log, a minute for a photo, and two minutes for a game. Use as much or as little as fits your day.

Are the games just for fun?

They're short, playful screen breaks and practice for things like following a moving dot or spotting small details. They're not a vision test and don't measure your eyesight. Many people use them as a two-minute pause between work sessions.

Trying it out6 questions
Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. The daily log, eye photos, the first three visual games in the app and the three browser games on this site are free, with no card needed. Ocufuse Plus is an optional upgrade with every game, your full history, all 12 guide modules and the Doctor Brief. Your data is never sold.

Do I have to download anything?

No. Ocufuse runs in your browser on a laptop, tablet or phone. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Can I try a game before I sign up?

Yes. The three browser games on this site need only your email, once per browser. Your best score stays in your browser.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Ocufuse runs in any modern browser on your phone or computer. Log in with the same account anywhere and your data is there. An iPhone app is on its way too.

Do I need any equipment?

No. Most games need only your screen. A few routines suggest something simple, like warm water and a clean cloth, and each one tells you before you start.

What if I miss a few days?

Nothing breaks. Your history waits for you, and you pick up where you left off.

Privacy and safety5 questions
Who can see my data?

Your log and photos are private to your account, stored in the EU, never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI. Nothing reaches a doctor unless you export a Doctor Brief yourself.

Is this a vision test or a medical app?

No. Ocufuse helps with habits, screen breaks and keeping a record to show your eye doctor. It does not diagnose or treat anything. For any eye concerns, see an eye care professional.

I'm not comfortable taking eye photos. Do I have to?

No. Photos are optional. You can use the games, the exercises and the daily log without ever taking one. If you do, they stay private to your account.

Can I use Ocufuse for lazy eye or strabismus exercises?

If your eye care professional has given you exercises, Ocufuse gives you guided steps, timers and a practice log to show them. It doesn't diagnose or treat amblyopia or strabismus, and games don't replace glasses, patching or therapy your clinician prescribes.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. Export your data or delete your account and everything in it from Settings, anytime, free.

Plus and billing4 questions
How do I cancel Ocufuse Plus?

In the web app: Profile and settings, then Ocufuse Plus, then Manage subscription. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Lifetime has nothing to cancel.

Will I be charged after the free trial?

Only if you keep Plus. You see the price and the date the trial ends before you start, and you can cancel before then in Manage subscription. If you cancel, you won't be charged.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. You keep the free plan with your recent history, and everything comes back if you return to Plus.

Can I get a refund?

Contact us and we handle refunds as set out in our terms.
