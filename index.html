How it works

Two minutes a day, one clear record

  1. Take guided photos

    Ocufuse walks you through four views of your eyes, the same way each day, so photos line up when you compare them later.

  2. Note what happened

    Log caffeine, sleep, screen time, eye drops and symptoms in a tap. Set when it happened: now, 15 minutes ago, an hour ago, or a time you pick.

  3. Compare and share

    Flip through days side by side, then export a Doctor Brief as print, text or CSV for your appointment.

Features

Built for the appointment, not the algorithm

Every feature serves one goal: giving you and your clinician a record that is consistent, dated and easy to read.

Compare any days

Grid, stack, or flip through an album with a slider. Overlay a reference photo to spot small shifts.

Context with timing

Each entry carries the time it happened. Repeated taps within ten minutes merge into one count.

Doctor Brief

A clean summary of your photos, symptoms and context, ready to print or send before a visit.

Daily checklist

See what is done and what is left today. The checklist folds away once you finish.

Practice

Short eye exercises and games, each with a plain description of what you do and how often you have done it.

Your language

English, Albanian, Turkish and Bosnian, with light and dark mode.

Privacy

Photos of your face are personal. We treat them that way.

Your log exists to help you and the people you choose to share it with.

  • Your photos and notes are never sold or used for advertising.
  • You decide what goes into a Doctor Brief and who receives it.
  • Export your data at any time.
  • Delete your account and everything in it from settings.

Ocufuse is a journaling tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. If your eyes change suddenly, hurt, or your vision gets worse, contact an eye doctor or emergency service.
