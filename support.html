Frequently asked questions

Questions about eye exercises, vision therapy or eye photos? Read all the answers.

What is Ocufuse?

An eye fitness and eye health journal. It has eye training games, guided eye exercises and comfort routines, lets you log how your eyes feel and add optional photos, and puts it together in a brief you can share with your clinician. It is not a medical device.

How much does it cost?

Ocufuse is free to start: logging, eye photos, your doctors, three visual games in the app and the three browser games on this site. Ocufuse Plus costs $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Plus is coming soon; during early access everything in the web app is free. Compare Free and Plus.

When is the iPhone app coming?

The App Store launch is planned for October 23, 2026. Join the waiting list on the iPhone app page. The web app is open now at app.ocufuse.com. When the iPhone app arrives, you'll sign in with the same account and your data will be there. Join the waiting list.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU, in Ireland. Photos are kept in private storage that only your signed-in account can read. Read the privacy policy.

Can Ocufuse tell me if my eyes are getting better or worse?

No. Ocufuse shows you your own photos and notes side by side, but it does not measure or judge them. Talk to your eye doctor about what you see. Safety and intended use.

Why do my photos look different from day to day?

Light, distance, head angle and the camera all change how eyes appear. Guided capture helps, but take photos in the same place and light each time for the most consistent results.

How do I share my journal with my doctor?

Open Brief, pick the dates, and export as print, text or CSV. Nothing is shared unless you export it yourself. What clinicians see.

How do I export or delete my data?

Exports are in the Brief section. To delete everything, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Full steps.

Which languages are supported?

English, Albanian, Turkish and Bosnian.
