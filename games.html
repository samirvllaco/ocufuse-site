Games for a screen break

Each game is short on purpose. Play one between tasks, then look away from the screen: the 20-20-20 rule suggests looking at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes. For the bigger picture, read screen eye strain: what actually helps.

Looking for eye exercises your orthoptist or optometrist gave you, such as the Brock string or pencil push-ups for convergence insufficiency? Those are in the Ocufuse web app as guided companions, and our eye exercise guides explain what the research says.
