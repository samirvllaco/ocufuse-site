Frequently asked questions

Pricing questions

Is Ocufuse really free?

Yes. Every feature in Ocufuse is free while it is in early access. You do not need a card to sign up, and nothing is charged automatically.

Will Ocufuse always be free?

Ocufuse is free during early access. If paid features are added later, you will see the price in the app before any charge, and you can decide then. Nothing changes for you without notice.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. You sign up with an email address, Google or Apple. Ocufuse does not ask for payment details.

Is there a plan for clinics?

Not yet. Clinicians do not need an account to read a Doctor Brief. If you would like to use Ocufuse with patients in your practice, email hello@ocufuse.com and tell us what would help.

How would I pay if paid features arrive?

On iPhone, any purchase would go through the App Store and Apple's standard terms. On the web, the price and payment method would be shown before you confirm anything.
