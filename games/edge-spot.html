Edge Spot: a peripheral vision game

A free peripheral vision game for a short screen break. Keep your eyes on the centre cross and spot the diamond that flashes near the edges. No download. Free with your email.

Edge Spot

Loading the game...

Ocufuse games are for fun and screen breaks. They are not a vision test and do not diagnose or treat any condition. See an eye care professional for any concerns.

How to play

  1. Press Start and look at the cross in the middle of the play area.
  2. Without moving your eyes, watch for the amber diamond that flashes near an edge.
  3. Tap that edge of the play area, or press the matching arrow key.

Levels and scoring

A game is 30 flashes in six levels of five, which takes about 90 seconds. Levels 1 and 2 use only the left and right edges; from level 3 all four edges are in play. Blue circles join as decoys from level 2, up to three at a time, and the flash gets shorter, from 0.7 seconds at level 1 to 0.2 seconds at level 6. You have 2.5 seconds after each flash to answer. A right answer scores 8 points plus 2 for each level, so 10 points at level 1 and 20 at level 6. Wrong or missed answers score nothing.

Tips for a high score

  • Keep your gaze on the cross. If your eyes jump to the edge, the flash is usually gone by the time they land.
  • Sit centred, so all four edges are about the same distance from your eyes.
  • On a large monitor, sit a little further back so the edges are not too far out.
  • Look for the colour first: the diamond is amber and the decoys are blue.
  • Blink between flashes. Staring without blinking dries your eyes out quickly.

Take a proper screen break

Edge Spot takes about a minute and a half. A game is a change of task, but your eyes are still on a screen. After a round, try the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for 20 seconds, and blink fully a few times. Our guide to the 20-20-20 rule explains where it comes from and what a 2022 study found, and screen eye strain: what actually helps covers screen setup, dry air and when to get your eyes checked.

Questions about Edge Spot

Is Edge Spot free?

Yes. All three browser games on ocufuse.com are free to play as often as you like, with no ads and no download.

Do I need an account to play?

No account. Just your email to unlock the three free games, once per browser. Your best score is saved in this browser. A free Ocufuse account at app.ocufuse.com keeps your practice history and app game scores on iPhone and web.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any up-to-date browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with touch, a mouse or a keyboard. The game pauses by itself if you switch tabs.

Is Edge Spot a peripheral vision test?

No. Edge Spot is a game, and a score cannot tell you anything about your field of vision. Screen size and how far you sit change it a lot. If you notice shadows, a curtain or missing areas at the side of your vision, see an eye care professional straight away.

Why does it matter where I look?

The game is about noticing things at the edge of your view while your eyes stay on the cross. If your eyes jump to the edges, the flash is usually gone before they arrive.
