Questions about Plus

Can I switch plans?

Yes. To move to Lifetime, pick it in the web app at any time; your subscription then stops renewing and ends with the period you already paid for. To switch between monthly and yearly on the web, open Subscription in the web app's settings; if the switch isn't offered there, contact us and we change it for you. On iPhone, switch in your Apple ID subscriptions.

What is the Lifetime plan?

A one-time payment for Ocufuse Plus with no renewals. It stays on your account on the web and iPhone. There is no free trial for Lifetime.

What happens when I cancel?

Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted: your logs, photos and doctors stay, and the free plan shows the last 14 days of history. Subscribe again later and your full history is back.

I subscribed on iPhone. Can I use Plus on the web?

Yes. Log in at app.ocufuse.com with the same account and Plus unlocks automatically. The same works the other way round for a web subscription.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial and the monthly plan with a 3-day free trial, once per account. You are not charged if you cancel at least a day before the trial ends. After that, the plan renews automatically until you cancel.

Can I get a refund?

For web subscriptions, contact us and we handle refunds as set out in our terms. Subscriptions bought on iPhone are billed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What stays free?

Health and safety logging, urgent help, eye photos, My doctors, export, account deletion, light and dark mode, sync between iPhone and web, and the three browser games on ocufuse.com are free for everyone.
