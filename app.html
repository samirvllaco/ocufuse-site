Questions

About the iPhone app

When does the iPhone app launch?

Ocufuse for iPhone is planned for the App Store on October 23, 2026. Join the waiting list and we will email you once, on launch day.

Do I need the iPhone app?

No. The web app at app.ocufuse.com works now in any modern browser on a phone or computer. The iPhone app adds a daily reminder and a phone-first layout, and uses the same account.

Will my entries carry over?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and everything you logged on the web is there on your iPhone, and the other way round.

Does Ocufuse Plus work on both?

Yes. Plus will be one subscription that unlocks on iPhone and web with the same account. Plus is coming soon; during early access everything in the web app is free.
